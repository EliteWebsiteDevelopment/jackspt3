Frequently Asked Questions

JBEE Electrical Contractors helps homeowners, businesses, property managers, and contractors make informed decisions about electrical systems, repairs, upgrades, and installations throughout Atlanta, GA.

What types of properties do you service?

We provide electrical services for residential, commercial, and industrial properties, including offices, retail spaces, warehouses, manufacturing facilities, and homes.

Do you provide emergency electrical repairs?

Yes. We offer prompt response services for electrical outages, failures, troubleshooting, and urgent repairs.

Can you handle electrical work for new construction projects?

Yes. We perform site development, underground electrical installation, rough-in work, finish-out services, and power distribution system installation.

Do you install EV charging stations?

Yes. We install residential, commercial, fleet, and multi-family EV charging solutions, including required circuit and panel upgrades.

Can you upgrade electrical panels and install generators?

Yes. We provide electrical panel upgrades, service entrance upgrades, transfer switches, standby generators, and backup power solutions.
